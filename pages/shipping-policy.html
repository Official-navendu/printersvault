Shipping & Delivery

Shipping Policy

Fast, reliable shipping for all your printing hardware and supply needs.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 PrintersVault Logistics Guidelines

1. Shipping Overview

At PrintersVault, we strive to deliver your desktop printers, heavy-duty commercial machines, ink cartridges, toner units, and accessories quickly and securely. All orders are carefully packaged to prevent damage during transit.

2. Free Shipping Offer

FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OVER $299
All orders with a subtotal of $299.00 or more (before discounts) qualify for FREE Standard Shipping. Orders under $299 ship at a low flat rate of $9.99.

3. Order Processing Times

Orders placed Monday through Friday (excluding public holidays) are typically processed and handed over to logistics partners within 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed during weekends or major holidays will begin processing on the following business day.

4. Shipping Methods & Delivery Estimates

Delivery options available at checkout include:

  • Standard Shipping: Estimated delivery within 3 to 5 business days following processing.
  • Express Delivery: Estimated delivery within 1 to 2 business days following processing.

Delivery timeframes are estimates provided by carrier partners and may vary slightly due to weather or seasonal shipping volumes.

5. Shipping Locations

PrintersVault ships to residential, business, and commercial addresses within the contiguous United States. Address accuracy should be verified at checkout to avoid delivery delays.

6. Order Tracking

Once your package is picked up by the logistics carrier, an automated email notification will be sent containing your shipment reference number and carrier tracking link. You can also view recent order statuses inside your Account Dashboard.

7. Delayed or Lost Shipments

If your package has passed the estimated delivery date without progress updates, please contact our support team. We will immediately liaise with the logistics provider to locate your shipment or issue a replacement if deemed lost in transit.

8. Incorrect Shipping Information

If you notice an error in your shipping address after placing an order, please email hello@printersvault.com immediately. While we attempt to intercept packages prior to dispatch, we cannot modify addresses once orders are in carrier custody.

9. Damaged Packages

If your shipment arrives with outer box damage, please take photos before opening the package and notify us within 48 hours of receipt. We will arrange a replacement or missing item reshipment promptly.
